How the model works

Reuses the PrizePicks AI 3.0 baseline engine: weighted averages of recent and up to 50 prior results, a simple normal approximation, minimum 12 games, stale-data rejection, and two-pick entry enumeration.

The normal model is not sport-specific, the probabilities are not calibrated, correlations beyond known same-game checks are not modeled, and payout/settlement rules are not implemented. The app does not fetch live lines or place entries.

iPhone installation

After hosting this app over HTTPS, open its URL in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. The interface shell may open offline, but predictions require an online backend.

PikBot is an independent research simulator and is not affiliated with PrizePicks.
